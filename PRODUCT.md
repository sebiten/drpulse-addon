# Product

## Register

product

## Users

WoW: Forever players who need to read and configure diminishing returns during PvP without leaving their gameplay flow.

## Product Purpose

Drpulse combines aura signals, casts, and native Blizzard data to display DR state on the current target and nameplates. Settings must make it clear what is tracked, what is displayed, and how it looks, with quick access through `/drpulse`.

## Brand Personality

Precise, tactical, and restrained. It should feel like a focused, reliable, and compact PvP tool.

## Anti-references

It must not copy Forever DR's panel layout, depend on a long accordion list, or resemble a generic Blizzard settings screen with only a superficial color change.

## Design Principles

- Prioritize frequent decisions and current state before advanced options.
- Visually separate appearance, detection, tools, and help.
- Show the effect of changes near each control through the existing previews.
- Keep useful information density without turning the page into an endless list.
- Use Drpulse's navy and cyan identity with functional restraint.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve native WoW controls and typography, communicate states through more than color, and use explanatory tooltips with clear click targets.
